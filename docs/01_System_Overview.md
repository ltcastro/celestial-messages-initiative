# CMI System Overview

Fictional Celestial Messages Initiative: members pay to encode a personal message onto a directed radio or laser beam aimed at a chosen star or cluster.

| Tier | Name | Nominal power | Monthly | Annual |
|------|------|---------------|---------|--------|

| 2 | Standard | 10 kW | $50 | $600 |
| 3 | Premium | 100 kW | $200 | $2,400 |
| 4 | Elite | 500 kW+ | $1,000 | $12,000 |

Daily message location in the database is elapsed / total. Real photons are a wavefront, not a package with a GPS fix.

## Laws and site

ITU Radio Regulations govern spectrum and space radiocommunication: allocation, coordination (Articles 9 and 11), no harmful interference, station identification, and national licensing (for example FCC Part 25 in the United States). A private high-power beam toward deep space is not unregulated just because the target is a star.

Preferred ocean site: remote high-salinity, low-RFI water. Mid-North Atlantic high-salinity region, or a quiet Pacific / Southern Ocean platform. Avoid shipping lanes.
